Gulf Job Seeker Guide

Practical checks before applying for a job or accepting an offer. This page is general guidance, not legal or immigration advice.

✓

Verify the employer

Find the company's official website and contact details independently. Confirm the vacancy through an official channel.

⚑

Watch for scams

Be suspicious of guaranteed offers, pressure tactics, personal-account payments and requests for sensitive documents before verification.

▤

Check the offer

Review job title, salary, location, working hours, benefits, contract terms and any stated expiry date in writing.

Before you apply

Use this checklist for every opportunity.

  • Do not pay a recruiter or an individual to guarantee a job offer.
  • Verify the employer and vacancy using contact details you find independently.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, bank PINs or unnecessary identity documents.
  • Confirm the recruitment process and any legally required fees through official government sources.
  • Do not assume that a job listing guarantees a visa, work permit or employment.
  • Keep copies of the written offer and communications.
  • Report suspicious job posts when a working reporting channel is available.
Country-specific rules change. Before acting, consult the relevant official government or labour authority website for the country and situation.
